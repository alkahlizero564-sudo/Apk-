# Espresso & caramel (demo of an AI-designed interface)
- Palette: background #120C08, surfaces #1C140E / #261B13, text #F7EDE2, muted #B09A86, caramel #C8873A, gold #F2C14E.
- Type: Readex Pro for text, Noto Kufi Arabic 900 for titles and numbers. Home title 44px with a cream→gold gradient.
- Shapes: radius 24 (cards), 28 (featured), 20 (thumbnails), round icons; thin gold borders at 12% opacity, glass blur.
- Home: transparent hero with a gold ring and a glossy coffee bean circle; tiles with gold numbers, the first tile filled
  with the caramel→gold gradient; featured cards 170px tall pictures, tilting with a soft glare.
- Lists: 70px thumbnails, gold prices, gold-tinted status pills, chips outlined, the active chip filled.
- Bottom bar: floating, 26px radius, dark glass, the active tab gold.
- Motion: cards rise 24px and fade in with a 60ms stagger (600ms, power3.out); featured cards tilt up to 8°.
